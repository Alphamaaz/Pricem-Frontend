**Effective Date:** 24 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Price Am ("Price Am", "we", "our", or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use our website and other applicable online services.

Cookies help websites function, remember preferences, understand how visitors use their services, and support security.

This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains how we collect and process personal information.

## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website or service. They may contain identifiers or other information that help a website recognise your browser or remember certain settings.

We may also use similar technologies, including local storage, pixels, tags, and software development tools, depending on the features implemented on Price Am.

These technologies may collect information about your device, browser, interactions with the platform, and preferences.

## 3. Types of Cookies We May Use

The cookies and similar technologies used on Price Am will depend on the services and features operating on the platform.

### 3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies support essential website functions, such as:

* Maintaining a secure session.
* Supporting account authentication.
* Protecting against certain security threats.
* Managing network traffic and technical stability.
* Remembering privacy and cookie consent choices.

These technologies are necessary for core functions and may not be available for disabling through our cookie preference tool. Where required by law, we will explain their use and obtain any necessary permissions.

### 3.2 Functional and Preference Cookies

These cookies may help remember choices such as language, display preferences, or other settings to make your experience more convenient.

If these cookies are not essential, we will seek consent where required by applicable law.

### 3.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies

If analytics tools are implemented, these cookies may help us understand how visitors use Price Am.

For example, they may help us assess:

* Which pages and product categories receive the most visits.
* How users navigate the marketplace.
* Whether visitors encounter technical errors.
* Which features may need improvement.

We will seek any consent required before using non-essential analytics cookies or similar tracking technologies.

### 3.4 Advertising and Marketing Cookies

If advertising or marketing technologies are introduced, they may be used to measure advertising performance, understand campaign interactions, or provide more relevant advertising.

Such technologies may be operated by third-party providers and may collect information through their own identifiers.

Where applicable law requires consent for these technologies, they will not be activated until the required consent has been obtained.

### 3.5 Third-Party Cookies

Some features may rely on third-party services, such as embedded content, analytics tools, security services, or advertising providers.

These providers may set cookies or use similar technologies according to their own policies. We will take appropriate steps to disclose relevant third-party technologies and manage consent where required.

The availability of a category in this Policy does not mean that every category is currently active on Price Am.

## 4. Why We Use Cookies

Depending on the technologies implemented, cookies may help us:

1. Operate essential marketplace features.
2. Maintain account sessions and improve security.
3. Remember preferences and settings.
4. Measure website performance and identify technical problems.
5. Understand visitor interactions and improve the user experience.
6. Support advertising measurement and marketing, where applicable and permitted.

We aim to use these technologies only for disclosed purposes and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

## 5. Your Cookie Choices

When required, Price Am will provide a cookie preference mechanism that allows you to accept or reject non-essential cookies and similar technologies.

You may also be able to change your choices later through a cookie settings link or tool available on the platform.

Strictly necessary technologies may remain active where they are required to provide a service you request or to maintain security, subject to applicable law.

Rejecting non-essential cookies should not prevent you from accessing core marketplace services unless a particular optional feature genuinely depends on the relevant technology.

## 6. Managing Cookies Through Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies through their settings.

Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

* Delete existing cookies.
* Block cookies from particular websites.
* Block third-party cookies.
* Receive notifications when cookies are set.
* Clear browsing data when you close the browser.

Please note that blocking certain cookies may affect login sessions, preferences, or other website functions.

Browser settings do not necessarily control every form of tracking technology, and deleting cookies may not automatically withdraw consent recorded elsewhere. Use the available Price Am cookie settings where appropriate.

## 7. Cookie Duration

Cookies may be either:

* **Session cookies:** These generally expire when you close your browser or when the relevant session ends.
* **Persistent cookies:** These remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them.

The duration depends on the purpose of the cookie and the configuration of the relevant service.

Where applicable, further details about active cookies, their purposes, providers, and expiry periods should be available through our cookie preference mechanism or accompanying cookie information.
