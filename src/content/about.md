# About Us – Price Am

## Welcome to Price Am

Price Am is an online marketplace designed to make buying and selling easier, more accessible, and more convenient. We bring buyers and sellers together on one platform where they can discover products, advertise items for sale, compare prices, and connect directly to complete their transactions.

Whether you are looking to buy something new, find a quality used item at a good price, or sell products you no longer need, Price Am provides a convenient place to get started.

Our marketplace is built around a simple idea: **making it easier for people to find what they need and for sellers to connect with potential buyers.**

## Our Marketplace

Price Am provides a marketplace for a wide range of products, helping individuals and businesses reach people who are interested in what they have to offer.

You can browse listings across different categories, including:

* **Electronics:** Mobile phones, laptops, accessories, and other electronic devices.

* **Fashion:** Clothes, shoes, bags, wigs, and fashion accessories.

* **Home and Living:** Furniture, household goods, and home accessories.

* **Personal Items:** A variety of everyday products and useful items.

* **Business and Commercial Products:** Goods offered by businesses, traders, and other commercial sellers.

* **New and Used Items:** Find brand-new products or explore pre-owned items that suit your needs and budget.

Our goal is to provide a marketplace where buyers have more options and sellers have an opportunity to reach a wider audience.

## How Price Am Works

Price Am connects buyers and sellers through product listings and listing-related communication.

**For buyers:** Browse available products, review listing descriptions and prices, ask sellers questions, and negotiate where appropriate before deciding whether to purchase.

**For sellers:** Create listings for products you want to sell, provide accurate information, upload clear photographs, set your asking price, and respond to enquiries from interested buyers.

Price Am serves as a marketplace for connecting buyers and sellers. Unless expressly stated otherwise for a particular service, users are responsible for agreeing on payment, delivery, collection, and other transaction arrangements directly with each other.

Price Am does not guarantee the quality, authenticity, condition, or availability of every item advertised by users. Buyers should independently verify important details before making payment.

## How to Sell on Price Am

Listing an item on Price Am is designed to be straightforward.

### 1. Prepare Your Item

Choose the item you want to sell and make sure you can accurately describe its condition. You can list eligible new or used products, whether you are an individual seller or a business.

### 2. Choose the Right Category

Select the category that best matches your product. Choosing the appropriate category helps potential buyers discover your listing more easily.

### 3. Add Clear Photos

Upload clear, genuine photographs of the item. Where possible, include multiple angles and close-up photographs of important features, accessories, or visible defects.

### 4. Write an Accurate Description

Provide useful details such as the product name, brand, model, condition, specifications, included accessories, and any known faults. Do not misrepresent the item or use photographs that create a misleading impression.

### 5. Set Your Price

Enter your asking price. Consider the item's condition, current market prices, and your preferred selling price. Where negotiation is available, you can discuss reasonable offers with interested buyers.

### 6. Publish Your Listing

Review your information carefully before submitting your listing. Make sure your price, description, category, and photographs are correct.

Once published, your listing can be viewed by potential buyers in the relevant marketplace categories.

### 7. Respond to Buyer Enquiries

Use Price Am's available listing-related communication features to answer questions, provide additional information, and discuss the transaction.

Keep conversations relevant to the advertised item and be cautious of anyone requesting unnecessary personal information or attempting to move the transaction into an unsafe arrangement.

### 8. Agree on Payment and Delivery

Before finalising a transaction, agree with the buyer on the price, payment method, delivery or collection arrangements, and any other relevant conditions.

Confirm payment independently before releasing your item. If delivery is arranged privately, use a method you trust and keep appropriate records of the transaction.

## Buying Safely on Price Am

Finding a product you like is only the first step. Taking reasonable precautions can help you make more informed purchasing decisions.

### Check the Listing Carefully

Read the description thoroughly and examine all available photographs. Ask the seller about the item's condition, age, specifications, functionality, and any defects that may affect your decision.

### Ask Questions Before Paying

Use the available communication features to clarify anything that is unclear. Be cautious if a seller avoids reasonable questions, provides inconsistent information, or pressures you to pay immediately.

### Verify the Item

Where practical, inspect the item in person before making payment. For electronics and other valuable products, test the important functions and check that the included accessories match the description.

If an in-person inspection is not possible, request additional photographs, videos, or other reasonable evidence of the item's condition.

### Be Careful with Payments

Avoid sending money solely because a seller claims an offer is limited or urgent. Be particularly cautious of requests for advance payment to an unverified seller.

Never share your banking PIN, password, one-time password (OTP), or other confidential financial credentials with another user.

Confirm the recipient and transaction details before authorising a transfer. Remember that a screenshot or payment receipt supplied by a buyer is not proof that funds have reached your account.

### Choose Safer Collection Arrangements

When meeting a seller, consider a public, well-lit location and take reasonable precautions when carrying cash or valuable items. For expensive purchases, consider bringing someone you trust.

If delivery is arranged, agree on the terms beforehand and use a delivery method you can verify.

### Report Suspicious Listings

If you encounter a listing that appears fraudulent, misleading, prohibited, or suspicious, report it through the available reporting features or contact Price Am customer support.

## Selling Safely on Price Am

Sellers should also take precautions to protect their products, personal information, and money.

### Describe Your Products Honestly

Provide accurate information about the item's condition, ownership where relevant, specifications, and any known defects. Honest listings help buyers make informed decisions and reduce disputes.

### Confirm Payments Before Releasing Items

Always check your bank account or payment service directly to confirm that funds have arrived. Do not rely on screenshots, SMS messages supplied by a buyer, emails, or other claims of payment without independent verification.

Never pay a buyer or a third party an unexpected fee to supposedly release funds from a transaction.

### Protect Your Personal Information

Share only the information necessary to arrange a legitimate transaction. Avoid publishing sensitive personal details in listing descriptions or sending confidential information to strangers.

Be cautious of anyone asking for your passwords, PINs, OTPs, or banking credentials.

### Watch Out for Suspicious Buyers

Be careful when a buyer offers to overpay, requests that you refund an alleged excess payment, sends suspicious payment confirmations, or asks you to follow unfamiliar payment links.

Do not release goods until payment has been independently confirmed and the agreed transaction conditions have been met.

### Keep Transaction Records

Where appropriate, retain relevant messages, payment confirmations from your own financial account, and delivery or collection records. These may be useful if a disagreement arises.

### Report Fraud and Suspicious Behaviour

If someone attempts to defraud you, impersonates another person, or engages in suspicious activity, stop the transaction and report the incident to Price Am.

## Our Approach to Safety and Security

At Price Am, we recognise that trust is important in an online marketplace. We aim to support a safer buying and selling environment through appropriate platform policies, user guidance, and measures to address suspicious activity.

Our security practices and safeguards may include the following, depending on the features available on the platform:

* **Listing moderation:** Reviewing or restricting listings that violate our marketplace rules.

* **Reporting mechanisms:** Providing ways for users to report suspicious listings, inappropriate conduct, or potential fraud.

* **Account protection:** Applying appropriate measures to help protect accounts and reduce unauthorised access.

* **Communication safeguards:** Encouraging users to keep item-related discussions relevant and to exercise caution when sharing contact or personal information.

* **Security monitoring:** Investigating credible reports of technical vulnerabilities, abuse, or suspicious platform activity.

* **Policy enforcement:** Taking appropriate action against accounts or listings that breach our rules, subject to applicable policies and procedures.

No online marketplace can eliminate every risk. Users should remain alert, independently verify important information, and avoid transactions that appear suspicious.

These measures describe our security objectives; specific protections depend on the features and controls implemented on the platform.

Buyers and sellers are responsible for verifying products and counterparties, agreeing on transaction terms, and choosing suitable payment and delivery arrangements.

We encourage users to communicate clearly, act honestly, and take reasonable precautions throughout every transaction.
